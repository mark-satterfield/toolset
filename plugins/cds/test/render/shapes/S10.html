How it works

Go from sign-up to first result in three steps

A short, ordered path. Each step builds on the one before it, so the sequence reads top to bottom on any screen.

  1. 1

    Connect your workspace

    Link the tools your team already uses. Read-only access is enough to get started, and you can widen scopes later.

  2. 2

    Set your guardrails

    Choose the rules that matter to you. Defaults are sensible, so most teams keep them and move on.

  3. 3

    Review your first result

    Within minutes you get a summary you can act on. Share it, refine the inputs, or schedule it to run on its own.
