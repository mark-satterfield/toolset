  1. 01 Connect

    Connect your sources

    Point Northwind at your warehouse, object storage, or a CSV upload. Schema is inferred on ingest and column types are validated before a single row is processed.

  3. 03 Ship

    Promote the winning model to a versioned REST endpoint with one click. Rollouts are gradual, every prediction is logged, and rollback is a single command away.

Support & answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything teams ask before rolling out the Northwind Acme Platform. Still stuck? Reach our team and we’ll get back within one business day.

How long does it take to get the platform running for my team?
Most teams are live within a single afternoon. You connect your identity provider, invite your first workspace, and the Acme Platform provisions a sandboxed environment automatically — no infrastructure to stand up. Larger Northwind-scale rollouts with custom SSO typically complete inside a week.
Where is my data stored, and who can access it?
Workspace data is encrypted in transit and at rest, isolated per tenant, and stored in the region you select during setup. Access is governed by role-based permissions that you control — admins can audit every action from the activity log and revoke access instantly.
Can I bring my own tools and integrate existing workflows?
Yes. The platform ships with a documented REST API, webhooks, and a CLI, plus prebuilt connectors for the tools most teams already run. If a connector doesn’t exist yet, you can script one against the public API in an afternoon — Globex built theirs over a lunch break.
What happens to my plan if my team grows mid-cycle?
Seats are prorated automatically. Add teammates whenever you need them and we’ll only bill the difference for the days remaining in your cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal, so you’re never charged for capacity you’ve stopped using.
Is there a free trial, and do I need a credit card to start?
Every workspace starts with a 14-day trial of the full feature set — no card required. You’ll get a reminder before it ends, and if you decide not to continue, your data stays exportable for 30 days afterward.
