Component group C6

Navigation & chrome

The persistent framing surfaces — the fixed topbar and its dropdown panels, the mobile drawer takeover, the scroll-aware sticky header, the application-shell left rail and its workspace switcher and account row, keyboard skip links, the multi-column footer, and the prev/next pagination strip. Fixed- and full-viewport components are rendered statically inside contained stages so every variant and interactive state is visible at once; in a live build they pin to the viewport, trap focus, and mount on demand.

C6.4 · §12.4

Sticky header

Editorial-only header that hides on downward scroll and reappears on upward scroll via transform: translateY(-100%) over 300ms. Inherits topbar dimensions. The hide is suppressed while focus is inside the header (WCAG 2.4.11). Two states shown — at rest, and translated off-screen.
