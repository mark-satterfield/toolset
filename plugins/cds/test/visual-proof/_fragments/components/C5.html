Component group C5

Overlays & menus

Six families that float above the page ground — modal dialogs, lightboxes, a non-blocking consent banner, a keyboard-first command palette, and the two trigger-driven menu surfaces (kebab and dropdown). Overlays are rendered in-place and statically here so every variant and interactive state is visible at once; in a live build they mount on demand and trap focus.

Overlays are statically positioned within bounded preview stages so every variant and state is visible at once. In a live build, dialogs, the lightbox, and the command palette mount on demand, lock body scroll, and trap focus; the consent banner stays non-blocking; menus open from their triggers and close on Escape or outside click.
