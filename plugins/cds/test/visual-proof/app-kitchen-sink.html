house-style

On-demand writing skill · v2.3.0

Enabled
Added by
You
Last updated
May 19, 2026
Trigger
Slash command + auto

Description

On-demand writing skill that encodes a personal voice, vocabulary, humor, and rhetorical techniques. Apply it to any prose drafted by hand or on your behalf — articles, posts, emails, memos, and docs. Does not auto-invoke unless a slash command is present.

Content

house-style

This skill encodes how a single author writes, so that anything produced on their behalf reads as though they wrote it themselves. It is organized as a set of layers, applied in order, from identity down to fine-grained mechanics.

Layer 1 — Identity (always on)

Write in the first person, plural only when speaking for a team. Favor short declarative sentences. Open with the claim, then defend it — never bury the lede beneath throat-clearing.

  • Prefer concrete nouns over abstractions; name the thing.
  • One idea per paragraph. If a paragraph runs past five sentences, split it.
  • Cut hedge words: basically, just, very, actually.

Layer 2 — Voice & humor

Dry, observational humor placed at the end of a section, never mid-argument. A joke should reward the reader who made it to the bottom — it should not interrupt someone scanning for the answer.

If a sentence makes you smile and also makes the point, keep it. If it only makes you smile, it belongs in the footnotes.

Layer 3 — Mechanics

  • Em dashes for asides — sparingly, no more than one per paragraph.
  • Oxford comma, always.
  • Numerals for anything countable above nine; spell out one through nine.
  • Code, file paths, and flags in monospace: --dry-run.

When invoked with /house-style, rewrite the supplied passage end-to-end. When triggered automatically, apply only Layers 1 and 3 and leave humor untouched unless explicitly asked.
