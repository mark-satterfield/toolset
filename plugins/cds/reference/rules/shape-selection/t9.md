# Shape selection — T9 Pricing

The pick follows the pricing model: subscription tiers with a segment toggle render as pricing-tiers; usage rates render as a rate table. Candidates are tried in order (primary, then alternates, then `default`) against the page constraints; if every candidate is rejected, the composer falls back to agent-generated layout and records that in the decisions sidecar.
