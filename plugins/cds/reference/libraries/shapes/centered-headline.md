# centered-headline — Centered marketing headline

A single headline centered horizontally on the page ground, standing on its own with no supporting elements.

## Determinations

- Type is Editorial Serif at ultralight weight 330, line-height 1.2; the size calibrates to 56px at the desktop reference viewport and 36px at mobile.
- The headline is centered on the page ground.
