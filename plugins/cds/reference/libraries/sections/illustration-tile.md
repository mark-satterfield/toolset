# Illustration tile

A tinted illustration tile between the article header and the body: one illustration whose ground is a saturated panel color from the `panels` palette. Its layout is the `full-width-tile` Shape (`libraries/shapes/full-width-tile.md`), which centers the illustration on the tinted ground.

## Determinations

- The tile is the only saturated panel surface on the page; every other Section stays on the principal editorial theme.
