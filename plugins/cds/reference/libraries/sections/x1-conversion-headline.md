# X1 — Conversion headline

The marketing headline standing outside the conversion card: a two-line serif statement above the card.

## Slots

- `headline` — a two-line marketing headline.

## Determinations

- Type is Editorial Serif at ultralight weight 330, line-height 1.2; the size calibrates to 56px at the desktop reference viewport and 36px at mobile.
- The headline is centered above the card on the page ground; no greeting copy ("Welcome back", "Sign in to continue") substitutes for it.
